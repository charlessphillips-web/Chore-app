Choreboard ("the app") helps people who share a house split chores fairly. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

**Who runs Choreboard:** [YOUR NAME], [CITY], United Kingdom. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL]

## What we collect

| Information | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Username | So housemates know who did what | Your housemates |
| Date of birth, gender, whether you're a student, university | To understand who uses the app (anonymous statistics for research and funding) | Only the app owner. Never shown to housemates |
| Chores you log, with photos | So housemates can check chores were done | Your housemates |
| Chores you flag as needing doing | To ask the right housemate | Your housemates |
| Votes on chore photos | To verify chores | Your housemates |
| Push notification token | To send you chore notifications | Not shown to anyone |
| Bug reports and suggestions (and any screenshot you attach), phone model and app version | To fix problems and improve the app | Only the app owner |

We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. You sign in anonymously; your account is tied to the app on your phone.

## Where it's stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase ([Firestore region, e.g. "europe-west2 (London)"]). Push notifications are sent through Expo's push service.

## How long we keep it

- Your profile and chores are kept while you use the app.
- If you **leave a house**, your membership and scores in that house are removed. Chores you logged stay in the house's history.
- If you **delete your account** (Settings → Delete my account), your profile is deleted straight away.
- Bug reports and suggestions are kept until they've been dealt with, and for no more than [12 months].

## Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. You can edit your details in Settings → Personal details, or email [YOUR EMAIL].

## Children

You must be 13 or over to use Choreboard.
